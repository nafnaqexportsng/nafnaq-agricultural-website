About NAFNAQ Agricultural

Quality Produce, Global Reach

NAFNAQ Agricultural connects international buyers with Nigerian agricultural products. Our focus is on quality sourcing, careful preparation, reliable supply and professional customer service.

Our specialty is Bitter Kola (Garcinia kola), while we also supply sesame seeds, hibiscus flower, cashew nuts, shea nuts and dried ginger.

Our Commitment

We are committed to supplying quality agricultural commodities from Nigeria while developing trustworthy relationships with local and international buyers.

For export enquiries, buyers can contact us with the product required, quantity and destination so that we can discuss availability, packaging, pricing and delivery arrangements.
